Why it matters

Chair cleaning

Hygiene and health

Cleaning removes bacteria, allergens and germs from the seats you use every day.

A longer chair life

Dust, dirt and stains wear the fabric down. Regular cleaning keeps chairs in good shape.

A better looking room

Clean chairs make a dining room, office or living room look neat and cared for.

Fresh, not stale

Chairs absorb smells from food, pets and use. Cleaning takes them out.

A good impression on guests

Clean seating shows attention to detail, at home or in a holiday rental.

Peace of mind

You know your space is hygienic and well kept.
