Why it matters

Sofa cleaning

Hygiene and fewer allergens

Deep cleaning removes the dirt, dust and allergens that build up in a sofa over time, so the air at home is cleaner.

A longer life for your sofa

Removing grit and surface stains protects the fabric from early wear, so your sofa stays in good shape for years.

A sofa that looks fresh again

Stains, discolouration and marks disappear and the colour comes back.

No more lingering odours

Pets, food and everyday use leave smells in the fabric. Cleaning takes them out instead of covering them.

Fabric and leather

We clean both, using the right method for each material so leather stays soft and fabric stays intact.

Peace of mind

A clean sofa means a clean, comfortable place for your family to relax.
