How it works

Our 6 step process

  1. 01

    Book a time slot

    WhatsApp us for a free quote and a date. We allocate 3 to 5 hours depending on the wardrobe size.

  2. 02

    Empty everything out

    Every item comes out so we start with a clean slate.

  3. 03

    Sort and decide

    Keep, donate or discard, item by item, together with you.

  4. 04

    Categorise by type

    Each group is placed logically for daily use.

  5. 05

    Clean the interior

    Shelves wiped down and dust removed before restocking.

  6. 06

    Restore and arrange

    Frequent items within reach, seasonal items stored away.
