FAQ

Questions clients ask us

Which areas do you cover?

The whole Costa del Sol: Málaga, Torremolinos, Benalmádena, Fuengirola, Marbella, Estepona, Benahavís, San Pedro de Alcántara, Nerja and more. If your town is not listed, message us anyway.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the item, size and fabric. Send us your list through the quote form and we reply with a price. The final amount may be adjusted once we see the furniture.

Is IVA included in the price?

Our prices are quoted without IVA. It is added to the final total.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. 5% for orders from 100€ to 199.99€, 10% from 200€ to 299.99€ and 15% for orders over 300€. The discount is applied automatically.

Can you clean leather furniture?

Yes. We clean both fabric and leather sofas, armchairs and chairs, including furniture on yachts.

Why do you prefer WhatsApp or email?

To avoid interruptions while working in a client home, we answer messages as soon as we finish a job. You always get a written reply with the details.

Do you also organise wardrobes?

Yes. We empty, sort, clean and rearrange your wardrobe at your home. A visit usually takes 3 to 5 hours depending on the size.
